Climbing Gym Tycoon Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Climbing Gym Tycoon ("the game") is made by Brock Rigby, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game uses, who receives it, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email brickrigby6@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in. Your gym is saved on your device, and I never receive it.
  • The game offers optional ads that you choose to watch for an in-game reward. You can play the whole game without watching any.
  • When you watch an ad, Unity's ad service and the ad networks it works with collect information about your device to show and measure the ad. They use your device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking.
  • I don't sell your information, and I don't collect it myself.

Information kept on your device

The game stores your saved gym, your settings, your tutorial progress, and how many ads you have watched today (to apply the daily limits). This stays on your device and is not sent to me. Deleting the game deletes it. If your device backs up app data (for example, to iCloud), your save may be included in that backup under Apple's terms.

Ads

Ads in the game are rewarded videos that play only when you tap a button to watch one. They are provided through Unity LevelPlay, a service of Unity Technologies (which includes ironSource), and the ad networks it works with to fill ads, such as Unity Ads and ironSource Ads.

When the game loads or shows an ad, these companies may collect:

  • your IP address, which they use to estimate your general location (such as country or city);
  • device identifiers: an identifier for this installation of the game, and your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • device information, such as the model, operating system, language, and network type;
  • information about the ads, such as which ads were shown, watched to the end, or tapped.

They use this to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, show ads based on your interests. How they handle this information is described in Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and in the privacy policies of the other ad networks involved.

I receive only summary reports from Unity, such as how many ads were watched and what they earned. These don't identify you.

Your choices about ads

  • Tracking (iPhone and iPad). The game asks whether it may track you before your advertising identifier is used. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still work but aren't personalized using that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EU, UK and similar regions). Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the game's menu.
  • In the ad. Many ads include a privacy icon where you can see your data, opt out of personalized ads, or ask Unity to delete what it has collected.
  • Not watching. Every ad is optional. If you never tap a "Watch ad" button, no ad is shown.

Purchases

If the game offers purchases, Apple processes them. I don't receive your payment details.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that sent their information to an ad partner, email me and I'll help get it removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or under California law), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to object to or limit its use, and to opt out of its being sold or shared for targeted advertising. Because I don't hold personal information about you, the quickest way to act on information used for ads is through the choices above or by contacting Unity at DPO@unity3d.com. You can also email me and I'll help you with your request. I don't sell your personal information.

How long information is kept

Information on your device is kept until you delete the game. Ad partners keep the information they collect for as long as their own privacy policies describe.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the change is significant, the game will tell you.
